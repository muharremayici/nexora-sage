# Nexora SAGE Licensing

Nexora SAGE 1.0.6 uses two alternative, unchanged public grants.

## Current Public Grants

You may rely on either grant when its complete terms cover your use:

- **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** permits the noncommercial uses,
  changes and distribution described by its official text. See
  `LICENSES/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md`.
- **PolyForm Free Trial License 1.0.0** permits evaluation for less than 32
  consecutive calendar days on behalf of you or your company. It does not
  permit distribution. See `LICENSES/PolyForm-Free-Trial-1.0.0.md`.

The grants are alternatives, not cumulative permissions. This summary does
not change or replace their terms.

## No Additional Offer

This release offers no additional paid or company-size-based grant, paid
support, consulting, contribution agreement, or automatic future-license
transition. Repository access, an issue, feedback, documentation, silence, or
technical capability does not create additional permission.

Future releases may use different terms only when those terms are explicitly
shipped with that future release. A future decision cannot revoke or narrow
rights already granted for a published release.

## Product Notice And Evidence Boundary

`NOTICE.md` explains SAGE-specific product, evidence, certification,
human-authority, target-repository and third-party boundaries. It is an

## Contributions

Issues, reproducible bug reports, design discussion and review feedback are
welcome. External copyrighted contributions, including code, tests, fixtures,
evaluation data and substantial documentation, are not currently accepted or
merged. See `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## Authority

`LICENSE` routes to the shipped grant texts. The applicable grant controls
over this explanation. Machine-readable SAGE contracts describe engineering
state and cannot create legal permission by themselves.
